Planning

Make the plan real.

A good plan starts with the real work, not with a date someone wants to hear.

What to do

  • Break down the work: list what actually needs to happen.
  • Name owners: every task needs one clear owner.
  • Save a baseline: otherwise you cannot measure drift.
  • Map dependencies: know what really drives the end date.
  • Update every 14 days: keep the plan alive.
