Benefits review

Check the value landed.

Finishing the project is not the same as proving the value happened.

What to check

  • What changed? Be clear what is different now.
  • By how much? Check the result against the baseline and target.
  • Who owns it now? Name the business owner who will keep tracking the value.
  • When will it be reviewed? Set a clear review date.
