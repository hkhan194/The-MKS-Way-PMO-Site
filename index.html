The MKS Way

The MKS Way helps us run projects clearly. It reduces confusion, late decisions, hidden risks, and poor handover.

What is the MKS Way?

The MKS Way is our shared way to start, run, and close projects at Moore Kingston Smith. It helps people use the same simple structure, so work is easier to explain, easier to govern, and easier to land properly.

It is there to give people the right amount of control, not extra admin. That means clearer ownership, earlier risks, and better decisions when they matter.

In simple terms

  • Start work clearly.
  • Keep delivery visible.
  • Finish with a proper handover.

Why it helps

Less confusion

Better handover

Projects finish with clear owners, open actions, and benefits that can be tracked.
