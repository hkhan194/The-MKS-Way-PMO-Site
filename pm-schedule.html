Planning

Keep the plan honest.

A plan helps only if it reflects the real work and keeps up with reality.

What to do

  • Build from the work: not from a preferred end date.
  • Save a baseline: so you can see drift.
  • Track dependencies: know what really drives the finish.
  • Update every 14 days: keep the schedule alive.
