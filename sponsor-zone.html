Sponsor zone

Own the outcome.

The sponsor owns the outcome. The project manager owns delivery.

What you own as sponsor

1Own the outcome

Keep the business result clear.

2Make key decisions

Step in on scope, money, and priority.

3Remove blockers

Clear senior obstacles fast.

4Back the project manager

Give one clear message and support.

5Own benefits after closure

Make sure the value actually lands.

What good looks like

  • You hear risks early, not for the first time when something breaks.
  • Your PM brings a recommendation, not just a problem.
  • You make decisions quickly and close the loop.
  • Benefit owners are named before the project closes.
  • You visibly back the change after go-live, not just at kick-off.

Two questions to ask at every board

  • What are the top three risks right now?
  • Is there anything you have not told me yet?

How to tell if the plan feels healthy

A good plan feels live, maintained, and honest.

  • There is a baseline.
  • You can see where today sits.
  • The end date is not drifting quietly.

Questions to ask before you say yes

A sponsor does not need all the detail. You do need the right three questions.

  • What will be measurably different?
  • By how much, and by when?
  • Who owns it after closure?

Benefits ownership

Benefits are the real improvements the project should deliver, such as saved time, lower risk, better service, lower cost, or stronger compliance.

As sponsor, you own making sure those benefits are defined, measured, and followed through after delivery.

Name the owner

Each benefit needs one business owner.

Agree the measure

Be clear how success will be checked.

Follow through

Keep tracking until the value lands.
