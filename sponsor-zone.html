Benefits ownership

Benefits are the measurable improvements the project is expected to deliver. This could be saved time, reduced risk, better client service, lower cost, improved compliance, or better ways of working.

Benefit ownership means someone is accountable for making sure those benefits are defined, tracked, and achieved after the project has delivered. The project team can help deliver the change, but the business owner must own the outcome.

Name the owner

Every key benefit needs one named business owner.

Agree the measure

Be clear how success will be checked and when it will be reviewed.

Follow through

Keep tracking after closure until the benefit has landed.
