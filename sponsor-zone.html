Sponsor zone

Own the outcome.

The sponsor owns the outcome. The project manager owns delivery.

Sponsors must do 5 things

  • Own the outcome.
  • Make key decisions.
  • Remove senior blockers.
  • Back the project manager.
  • Own benefits after closure.

Sponsor Charter

  1. Be available for decisions.
  2. Make the decisions only you can make.
  3. Say the same thing as your project manager.
  4. Tell bad news early.
  5. Show up at the moments that matter.

Two questions to ask at every board

  • What are the top three risks right now?
  • Is there anything you have not told me yet?

Benefits ownership

Benefits are the good things the project should deliver. They need an owner after closure.

  • Name the benefit owner.
  • Agree how it will be measured.
  • Check after the project closes.
