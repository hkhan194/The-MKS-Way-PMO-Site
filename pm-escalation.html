Escalation

Raise problems early.

Escalation is there to get help or a decision before the project loses control.

When to escalate

  • A Red risk needs senior help.
  • A decision is late.
  • A key dependency is failing.
  • The project is turning Amber or Red.

What to include

  • What the problem is.
  • What it means for time, cost, scope, or outcome.
  • What you have already tried.
  • What you need now.
  • Your recommendation.
