Gateway meetings

Use gateways to make clear decisions.

A gateway is a short decision point. It checks whether the project is ready to move on.

What happens in a gateway meeting?

  • Check the right work is done: look at what should be complete for that stage.
  • Review the live picture: risks, issues, dependencies, actions, and decisions.
  • Test readiness: confirm whether the next stage is clear, funded, and owned.
  • Agree next actions: name owners and dates for anything still open.
  • Make the decision: continue, pause, redirect, or stop.

How to get ready for a gateway

StageBring to the meetingBe ready to answer
DraftProposal form, sponsor name, problem to solve.Why is this needed and should it become a project?
PlanningPlan, scope, RAID, owners, dates.Is the plan realistic and understood?
DeliveryStatus, changes, open risks, key decisions.Is delivery under control and does anything need escalation?
