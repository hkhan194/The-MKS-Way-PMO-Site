Project controls

Keep the real picture visible.

The job is to show the real state of the project while there is still time to act.

What to keep live

  • Risk: something that might happen.
  • Issue: something that has happened.
  • Assumption: something treated as true but not confirmed.
  • Dependency: something you need from someone else.
  • Status: what moved, what is blocked, and what needs a decision.
