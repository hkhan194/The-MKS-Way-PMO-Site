Project lifecycle

See the full journey.

A project moves through clear stages. Each stage answers a simple question before the project moves on.

Project stages

StageWhat happensGateway
Stage 1: InitiationSet the goal, name the sponsor, and agree the brief.Gateway 1
Check the brief is clear and the project is ready to plan.
Stage 2: PlanningBuild the plan, start the RAID log, and confirm owners and dates.Gateway 2
Check the plan is realistic and key risks are understood.
Stage 3: DeliveryRun the work, report honestly, and control changes.Gateway 3
Check delivery is under control and the work is ready to close.
Stage 4: ClosureHand over properly, close actions, and confirm what has landed.Gateway 4
Check closure is complete and benefit owners are in place.
Stage 5: Benefits / ReviewReview whether the benefits happened and what to improve next time.Review whether the benefits landed after the project has closed.

What happens at a gateway meeting?

  • Check the work: make sure the right work has been completed.
  • Review the live picture: confirm risks, issues, dependencies, and decisions.
  • Decide on the next stage: agree whether the project can move forward.
  • Lock in actions: confirm owners, dates, and next steps.
  • Make the call: stop, pause, redirect, or approve continuation if needed.
