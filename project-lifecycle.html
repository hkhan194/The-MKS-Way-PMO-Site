What matters most

Make the plan real

Build from the work, not from a date someone hopes for.

  • Break down the real work.
  • Save a baseline.
  • Update it every 14 days.

Use gateways to decide

Gateway meetings are there to make a clear call, not just review a pack.

  • Check readiness.
  • Review risks and decisions.
  • Agree the next move.
