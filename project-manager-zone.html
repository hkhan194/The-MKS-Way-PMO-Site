Project manager zone

Keep work moving.

The project manager keeps the work organised, visible, and moving.

Do this next

  • Keep owners and dates clear.
  • Keep RAID and status honest.
  • Escalate early when stuck.

PM role

You do not need to know every answer. You need to keep owners, dates, risks, and decisions visible.

RAID

TermPlain meaning
RiskSomething that might happen.
IssueSomething that has happened.
AssumptionSomething treated as true but not yet confirmed.
DependencySomething needed from another person, team, supplier, or project.

Reporting

Report what is true. Show progress, risks, decisions, and next steps.

Escalation

Escalate when a blocker needs help.

  • Red risk, missed decision, or budget pressure.
  • Scope change or key dependency failing.
  • Sponsor not responding, or project going Amber or Red.

Useful files

  • RAID Log
  • Status Report
  • Escalation Brief
Need more detail?
  • Planning and scheduling: break work into tasks, name owners, use honest dates.
  • Change control: use it when scope, cost, time, or benefits change.
  • Lessons learned: capture what helped and what to do better next time.
  • Closure checklist: confirm what landed, hand over actions, name benefit owners.
