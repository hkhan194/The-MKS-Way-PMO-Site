PM role

You do not need to know every answer. You need to keep the work clear, visible, and moving.

Set the shape of the work

Keep the goal, scope, and deliverables clear.

Run a realistic plan

Break the work down, set honest dates, and name owners.

Keep risks visible

Track risks, issues, assumptions, and dependencies before they surprise people.

Report the truth

Show progress, blockers, decisions, and what needs help.

Control change

Spot when scope, time, cost, or benefits are shifting.

Close properly

Hand over clearly and make sure actions still have owners.

RAID

RAID is a simple working list that helps you keep the real picture in view.

TermPlain meaning
RiskSomething that might happen.
IssueSomething that has happened.
AssumptionSomething treated as true but not yet confirmed.
DependencySomething needed from another person, team, supplier, or project.

Reporting

Good reporting is short, honest, and useful.

  • Show progress: say what has moved forward since the last update.
  • Be clear on status: use Amber or Red early, not after the problem has grown.
  • Call out decisions: say what needs approval and by when.
  • Make next steps visible: keep owners and dates easy to see.

Escalation

Escalate when the project needs help that the team cannot sort out by itself.

Spot the problem

A decision is late, a risk turns Red, or a blocker will hit time, cost, scope, or delivery.

Say what it means

Be clear on impact, urgency, and what happens if nothing changes.

Take it to the right level

Raise it to the sponsor, board, or PMO with a clear ask.

Track the outcome

Confirm the decision, owner, and next action.

  • Escalate when: a risk is Red, a decision is missed, or the budget is under pressure.
  • Escalate when: scope is changing, a key dependency is failing, or the sponsor is not responding.
  • Escalate when: the project is moving to Amber or Red and needs senior help.
