Project manager zone

Keep work moving.

The project manager keeps the work organised, visible, and moving.

Your job as PM

You do not need all the answers. You need to keep the work clear, honest, and moving.

1Set the shape

Keep the goal, scope, and deliverables clear.

2Run the plan

Set honest dates and name owners.

3Keep risks visible

Track what could block the work.

4Report the truth

Show the real picture, not the easy picture.

5Close properly

Hand over clearly and close actions.

What good looks like

  • RAID is live and changes as the project changes.
  • The schedule is updated at least every 14 days.
  • Owners, dates, and next actions are easy to see.
  • Issues are logged quickly, not remembered later.
  • Escalations come with a clear recommendation.

What you need to stay on top of

Keep the real picture visible

Raise problems early

Escalation is part of good control, not a sign of failure.

  • Say what the problem is.
