Team member / SME zone

Know what you own.

Team members do not need to run the project. They need to own their tasks, raise issues early, and be clear about blockers.

You own 3 things

1The work you agreed to do.
2Honest updates on progress and blockers.
3Early warning when something is at risk.

Do not worry about

  • Project governance.
  • Sponsor decisions.
  • Full project reporting.

Raise blockers

  • Say what is blocked.
  • Say what help you need.
  • Tell the project manager early.

Give estimates

Give the best honest view you have, not the answer you think people want.

  • Say how long your work should take: use a realistic date based on what you know now.
  • Say what could change it: for example waiting on data, system access, reviews, or another team.
  • Update it early: if your estimate changes, say so before the deadline slips.

How to spot and flag risks early

A risk is something that might happen. Raise it before it becomes an issue.

Spot the signal

A task depends on someone else, the date feels tight, or the change may not land well in your area.

Say why it matters

Explain what could happen if nothing changes.

Raise it early

Tell the project manager while there is still time to act.

Support change

Your input helps the change work in real life, not just on paper.

Share practical feedback

Point out what will work well and what may cause problems in your team.

Help test the change

Check new steps, forms, reports, or systems before they go live.

Support handover

Help people understand the new way of working and what they need to do next.
