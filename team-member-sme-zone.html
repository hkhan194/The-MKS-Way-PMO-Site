Team member / SME zone

Know what you own.

Team members do not need to run the project. They need to own their tasks, raise issues early, and be clear about blockers.

You own 3 things

1The work you agreed to do.
2Honest updates on progress and blockers.
3Early warning when something is at risk.

Do not worry about

  • Project governance.
  • Sponsor decisions.
  • Full project reporting.

How to help the project

Raise blockers early

Say what is blocked, why it matters, and what help you need while there is still time to act.

Give honest dates

Use realistic estimates and say early if something is likely to slip.

Help the change land

Share practical feedback, help test the change, and support handover in your area.

A useful update sounds like...

  • 'This task is blocked because...'
    Say what has stopped it.
  • 'I can still hit Friday if...'
    Say what needs to happen to stay on track.
  • 'There is a risk this slips because...'
    Raise the warning before it becomes an issue.

Tell the PM early when...

  • You are waiting on another team.
  • You do not have the information or access you need.
  • A review or approval is late.
  • Your date is starting to slip.

Risk or issue?

Risk

Something that might happen.

Example: There is a risk a supplier review will be late and delay testing.

Issue

Something that has already happened.

Example: The supplier review is already late and testing cannot start.
