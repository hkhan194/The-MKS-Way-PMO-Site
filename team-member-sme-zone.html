Team member / SME zone

Know what you own.

Team members do not need to run the project. They need to own their tasks, raise issues early, and be clear about blockers.

You own 3 things

  • The work you agreed to do.
  • Honest updates on progress and blockers.
  • Early warning when something is at risk.

Do not worry about

  • Project governance.
  • Sponsor decisions.
  • Full project reporting.

Raise blockers

  • Say what is blocked.
  • Say what help you need.
  • Tell the project manager early.

Give estimates

Give the best honest view you have. Say what could change it.

How to flag risks early

A risk is something that might happen. Raise it before it becomes an issue.

Support change

  • Explain what will work in your area.
  • Help test the change.
  • Support handover.

Useful files

  • Status Report
  • RAID Log
  • Stakeholder Engagement Plan
